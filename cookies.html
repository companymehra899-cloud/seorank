Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how OptiRank uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our websites and use our platform.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They help websites function, remember preferences, and understand usage patterns.

Categories we use

CategoryPurposeRequired
Strictly necessaryAuthentication, security, load balancing, and consent storageYes
PerformanceAggregated analytics such as pages viewed and errorsNo
FunctionalRemembering language, region, and dashboard preferencesNo
MarketingMeasuring campaign performance and relevanceNo

Managing preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings and clear cookies at any time. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect core functionality. You can also dismiss the cookie banner on this site, which stores a preference in local storage rather than a tracking cookie.

Third-party cookies

Some features rely on third parties such as analytics and payment processors, which may set their own cookies. Their use is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Do Not Track

Our websites do not currently respond to Do Not Track signals because there is no industry consensus on how to interpret them.

Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in technology or regulation. The effective date at the top will be revised accordingly.
