Privacy Statement

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This Privacy Statement explains how OptiRank Inc. ("OptiRank", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use our websites, platform, APIs, and related services (the "Services").

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, work email, company, password hash, and role.
  • Billing data: plan, billing address, and payment processor tokens. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, features used, device and browser type, IP address, and timestamps.
  • Content data: projects, keywords, domains, and reports you create in the Services.
  • Communications: messages you send to sales or support.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Services.
  • Operate free tools without requiring an account.
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Send service notices, security alerts, and, with consent, marketing communications.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent, and legal obligation. You can withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis.

4. Cookies and tracking

We use essential cookies to operate the Services and analytics cookies to understand usage. See our Cookie Policy for details and how to manage preferences.

5. Sharing information

We share personal information with service providers that help us run the business (hosting, analytics, payments, email). We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. International transfers

We may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for contact, newsletter, or legal, accounting, and reporting requirements.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection to processing. California residents have rights under the CCPA, including the right to know and delete, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. To make a request, contact privacy@optirank.io.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and monitoring. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes to this statement

We may update this Privacy Statement. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
