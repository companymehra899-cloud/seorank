Pricing FAQ

Do I need an account to use the tools?

No. Rank tracking, audits, keyword research, backlinks, competitor analysis, and AI visibility are open with no signup or login. Paid plans are for teams that want higher limits and API access.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

You can cancel anytime. Payments are non-refundable except where the service was not delivered as promised. Contact support and we will review your case.

Can I get the annual plan but pay monthly?

Annual plans are billed upfront to provide the discounted rate. Contact sales to discuss options such as a quarterly payment schedule with an annual commitment.

Can multiple people use the tools together?

Yes. Tools are open with no login. For paid plans, add seats so each teammate has their own access.

Do you offer custom plans?

Yes. Custom plans are available if you need limits beyond the standard tiers. Reach out to sales and we will scope the right option for your team.
